# Data Access Statement

The study integrates publicly available pharmacovigilance resources together with restricted healthcare-derived datasets.

Publicly available resources include:

- FAERS
- WHO VigiAccess
- EudraVigilance
- PubChem
- RCSB Protein Data Bank

Certain UAE healthcare datasets cannot be redistributed because of:

- institutional agreements
- regulatory restrictions
- third-party ownership
- privacy requirements

Therefore, restricted datasets are not included within this repository.

Researchers wishing to reproduce the study should obtain equivalent authorized datasets through the appropriate data providers.
