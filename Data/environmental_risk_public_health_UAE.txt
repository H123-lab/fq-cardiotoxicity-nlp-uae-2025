Environmental Context for Precision Pharmacovigilance

Purpose

This document summarizes the role of environmental information within the analytical framework of this study.

Role in the Study

Environmental information was incorporated exclusively to provide contextual support for geospatial pharmacovigilance interpretation within a broader One Health framework.

Environmental information was not used:

to estimate causal associations;
to calculate environmental exposure risk;
to model population incidence;
to predict individual cardiotoxicity.

Accordingly, environmental information should be interpreted as hypothesis-generating rather than inferential.

Analytical Scope

The primary analytical framework comprised:

pharmacovigilance signal detection;
clinical risk prediction using explainable machine learning;
natural language processing;
regional pharmacovigilance visualization;
molecular docking and computational pharmacology.

Environmental context was considered only during interpretation of regional reporting patterns.

Interpretation

Because environmental monitoring data, spontaneous adverse-event reporting systems, and healthcare utilization represent distinct surveillance systems, no direct causal relationships between environmental fluoroquinolone concentrations and cardiovascular adverse drug reactions were inferred.

Future studies incorporating prospective environmental sampling together with individual-level clinical exposure data may further clarify potential relationships.
